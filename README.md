Dishcision
What are we eating?

Dishcision takes the guesswork out of everyday meals.

Add the food you already cook, keep it available in your personal food library and let Dishcision make the decision for you.

No endless scrolling.
No complicated recipes.
No unfamiliar ingredients.

Just a practical meal decision based on what you already have.
